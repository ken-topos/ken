## Objective — ✅ **operator-COMMITTED scope**

Typed IPv4/IPv6/Unix addresses; stream and datagram kinds; bounded
send/receive; accept/connect/listen/shutdown; socket-error context; **explicit
socket option families rather than integer pairs**; and an **injected resolver
capability whose trust source and policy are visible.**

★ **DNS is a service boundary, not a syscall.** That is why the resolver is an
injected capability rather than a function: its trust source is a policy
decision the program must be able to see and choose.

⛔ **Option families, not integer pairs.** `(level, optname)` integer pairs are
an untyped hole through the capability surface.
